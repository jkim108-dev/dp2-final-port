Kinetic installation · 2025 · Collaboration

Spin
and Stop.

Time keeps moving.
We don’t always move with it.

Two roller-based objects hold two different rhythms of life. One carries words forward through motor-driven motion. The other lets its paper fall loose and remain still.

Explore the work ↓

Format

Collaborative kinetic installation

Elements

Metal, paper, rollers & motor

Inquiry

Time, repetition & stillness

01 / The idea

One passing moment.
Two ways of being.

We often measure life by how much moves forward. Yet the passing of time and our capacity to move with it are rarely the same.

In Spin and Stop, short reflections on life travel along a paper strip. Each phrase appears, passes through view, and disappears, giving time a visible, readable form. Beside it, loose paper interrupts that steady progression. The words remain, but their movement has paused.

The two objects make room for both experiences: the rhythm that carries us onward, and the moments when we need to stop within it.

State 01 / Spin

A sentence becomes a moment.

The motor sets a continuous pace. Reading becomes an encounter with something already passing: a phrase can be noticed, held briefly in thought, and then lost from view. Rotation gives an ordinary strip of paper the duration of a lived moment.

State 02 / Stop

A pause still holds a life.

The second object leaves its paper loose and still. Without the pull of forward motion, the strip becomes a visible weight and a physical presence. This pause invites a different kind of attention: to waiting, to rest, and to what remains when progress is suspended.

02 / Material & mechanism

The mechanism
stays visible.

Exposed bearings, rollers and metal joints make the source of movement legible. Against this rigid structure, paper carries the more fragile element: language. Directional light draws a second set of circles and lines in shadow, extending the work beyond its physical edges.

03 / A shared rhythm

What does it mean
to keep going?

Seen together, the objects make movement and stillness equally present. A viewer can follow the text, wait for the next phrase, or linger with the paper that has stopped.

The work leaves that question open. Life continues to unfold, while each person meets its rhythm differently.

04 / From the same exhibition

The Spin Never Stops,
Yet We Remain Seated.

Presented in the same exhibition, this companion work approaches the rhythm of life through a rotating form and the image of remaining seated. Together, the two projects explore the distance between a world in motion and a person’s own pace.
